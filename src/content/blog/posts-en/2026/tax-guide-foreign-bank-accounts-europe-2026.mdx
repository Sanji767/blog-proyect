# Tax Guide for Foreign Bank Accounts & Interest in Europe (2026)

Holding an account with an **international IBAN** (such as Trade Republic with DE IBAN, Revolut with LT IBAN, or Wise with BE IBAN) is fully legal under EU free movement of capital rules. However, understanding tax reporting rules for foreign interest income is essential for peace of mind.

---

## 📌 1. CRS (Common Reporting Standard) & Automatic Exchange

Under European Union directives and CRS rules, European banks automatically exchange financial account information annually with the tax authority of your country of tax residence.

---

## 📑 2. Declaring Foreign Interest Income

Unlike local banks that withhold income tax at source, foreign digital banks generally pay interest in full (gross). You are responsible for reporting gross annual interest earned in your annual personal tax return under investment/savings income.
