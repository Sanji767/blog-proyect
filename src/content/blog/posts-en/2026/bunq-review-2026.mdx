# bunq Review 2026: The Best Savings Rate of Any Neobank — If You Read the Fine Print

bunq is the Dutch bank that calls itself "the bank of The Free", and in 2026 it has one of the strongest pitches in European digital banking: a genuinely free plan, a savings rate of up to 3.11%, local IBANs in seven countries, and a student deal that gives away its €9.99-a-month Pro plan for nothing. But the headline rate comes with conditions that most summaries skip, and the old Easy Bank and Easy Bank Pro plan names are gone, replaced by Free, Core, Pro and Elite. This review covers what bunq actually costs and pays in 2026. The [bunq profile](/en/programas/bunq) has the essentials on one page: plans, IBANs, fees and how to open an account.

## What bunq is

bunq is a fully licensed Dutch bank — bunq B.V., supervised by De Nederlandsche Bank (DNB) and the Authority for the Financial Markets (AFM) — founded in Amsterdam in 2012. That matters: unlike e-money institutions, bunq holds a full banking licence, and [eligible deposits are covered up to €100,000 per account holder by the Dutch Deposit Guarantee Scheme](https://www.bunq.com/en-us/personal/plans). It also runs a registered German branch, so customers who sign up as Germany residents get a German IBAN while remaining under Dutch deposit protection.

bunq has four personal plans. [Free costs €0, Core costs €3.99, Pro costs €9.99 and Elite costs €18.99 per month](https://www.bunq.com/en-us/personal/plans). New customers get 30 days of Elite free on sign-up, then choose a plan.

**Free** is a real account, not a demo: three bank accounts, a digital card, and the same headline savings rate as the paid plans. Its limits are the €1,000-a-year allowance for foreign-currency spending at the lower FX rate and no free ATM withdrawals or physical card.

**Core (€3.99)** adds two more accounts, a physical card and unlimited foreign spending at the 0.5% rate. **Pro (€9.99)** is the budgeting plan: 25 accounts each with its own IBAN, three physical cards, six free cash withdrawals a month and five free foreign-currency transfers. **Elite (€18.99)** adds worldwide travel insurance, ten free foreign-currency transfers, the lowest stock-trading fees and eSIM data. A metal card costs €99 on Pro or Elite, and a bunq Pack puts up to four people on Pro for €28.99 a month — €7.25 each.

## The student deal: Pro free

This is one of the best student banking offers in Europe. [Under 25 and at university? Get bunq Pro free, or bunq Elite for €9 instead of €18.99](https://www.bunq.com/en-us/personal/plans). [Students in Belgium, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, the Netherlands, Portugal and Spain can use bunq Pro — worth €9.99 a month — for free, as long as they provide proof of enrolment](https://www.bunq.com/en-es/personal/use-cases/students). You upload proof of enrolment through in-app support and the discount applies automatically. If you are a student in one of those eight countries, there is little reason to pay for a bank account anywhere else.

## Savings: up to 3.11%, with a catch

The headline is real — bunq's plans page lists up to 3.11% interest on savings on every plan, including Free — but the rate is a base rate plus a bonus rate, and which one you get depends on your balance history. As read on bunq's own pages in November 2026: [everything up to a threshold earns 1.61% base interest (1.51% on the part above €10,000) and everything above it earns 3.11% bonus interest, credited weekly](https://freenance.io/savings/bunq-easy-savings-2026-review-3-percent-eur-deposit-protection/). The threshold resets on 1 January and 1 July to your highest balance of the previous six months, and from a first deposit until the next reset, all of your savings earn the bonus rate.

What that means in practice: a new saver sees 3.11% on everything, because the threshold starts at zero. A saver whose balance has stopped growing drifts toward the base rate of 1.61% on the first €10,000 and 1.51% above it. The 3.11% is therefore best understood as a rate on *new* money and growing balances, not a flat rate on your whole savings forever. Even the base rate of 1.61% is respectable by 2026 standards. Just do not budget on 3.11% on a static balance.

The rate is variable and moves roughly in line with ECB policy, so check the live rate in the app before moving a large sum across.

## Local IBANs in 7 countries

One of bunq's biggest practical advantages over N26 and Revolut is that [you can get a local bank account with an IBAN from the Netherlands, Germany, France, Spain, Belgium, Italy or Ireland](https://www.bunq.com/?no_redirect=true). Sign up as a resident of Germany and you get a DE IBAN, which means salary payments, rent and SEPA direct debits work exactly as they would from a German bank — no IBAN-discrimination friction with employers or insurers. Each of your up to 25 accounts on Pro and Elite has its own IBAN, so you can give your landlord one IBAN, your employer another, and run direct debits from a bills account without touching your spending money.

## Foreign currency: the 0.5% behind "ZeroFX"

bunq advertises "ZeroFX card payments abroad" on Core, Pro and Elite, and up to €1,000 a year on Free. The price sheet tells a more precise story: [foreign-currency card payments are charged at the Mastercard rate plus 0.5% on all plans, and on Free the rate rises to 1.5% after €1,000 a year](https://www.bankverse.ch/bunq-review/). The 0.5% is modest — far below the 2–3% traditional banks charge, and competitive with most neobanks — but it is not zero, and it is worth knowing before you compare bunq with N26, which charges no markup at all on card payments.

On ATM withdrawals, the first 6 a month are free on Pro and Elite, the next 5 cost €0.99 each, and €2.99 each after that. Free and Core customers pay for withdrawals, so bunq Free is a poor choice if you need regular cash.

## Investing, cards and extras

bunq lets you invest in stocks from the app, with fees of [0.99% per transaction on Free and Core, 0.79% on Pro and 0.49% on Elite](https://www.bankverse.ch/bunq-review/). That is expensive compared with a low-cost broker — a €1,000 stock purchase costs €9.90 on Free — so treat it as a convenience feature for small amounts, not a replacement for a brokerage account.

The app's budgeting automation is genuinely strong: automatic sorting of income into sub-accounts, auto-savings rules, spending insights and invoice scanning on Pro. bunq also plants trees as you spend — a nice touch, not a reason to choose a bank.

## Honest downsides

- **The 3.11% is a bonus rate, not a flat rate.** Once your balance stops growing, most of it earns the 1.61% base rate. Still decent — but the headline oversells it.
- **0.5% FX markup on card payments.** Small, but N26 charges nothing and Wise charges less on many routes. Heavy foreign spenders will notice it.
- **No free ATM withdrawals on Free and Core.** If you need cash regularly, the free plan is not really usable as a main account.
- **Paid plans are expensive for what they are.** €9.99 a month for Pro is fair if you use 25 accounts and the automation; €18.99 for Elite is hard to justify unless you use the travel insurance and eSIM.
- **Stock trading is pricey.** 0.99% per order is several times what a low-cost broker charges. Do not build a portfolio in bunq.
- **Plan names and prices change often.** The Easy Bank branding of a few years ago is gone, prices have risen, and the savings-rate structure has been reworked. Re-check terms yearly.
- **No branches, app-only support.** Like all neobanks, there is no office to walk into when something goes wrong.

## Who should open bunq in 2026?

bunq is the best neobank in Europe for two groups: **students under 25** in the eight eligible countries, who get Pro free with 25 IBANs and up to 3.11% savings — an unbeatable deal — and **savers who add money regularly**, who will actually earn the 3.11% bonus rate on new deposits. It is also the strongest option if you need a local IBAN in a country where you are not a citizen, or if you run your budget across many sub-accounts.

It is a weaker choice as a pure travel card, where N26's zero FX markup wins, and as a main account on the Free plan if you need cash often. The practical setup: open Free, try the 30 days of Elite, and let the savings rate and the local IBAN do the work. If you are a student, claim Pro free on day one.
