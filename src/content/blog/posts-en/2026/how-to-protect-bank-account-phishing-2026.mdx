# How to Protect Your Bank Account from Phishing and Mobile Scams (2026)

Digital banking security is more critical than ever. Phone spoofing (vishing) and SMS phishing (smishing) have grown increasingly sophisticated.

---

## 🛡️ The 3 Golden Rules of Banking Security

1. **Your bank will NEVER ask for passwords over the phone:** Neither PIN numbers, one-time passwords (OTP), nor SMS verification codes.
2. **Beware of urgent "safe account" transfer requests:** If someone calls claiming your account is compromised and demands you transfer funds to a "safe holding account", hang up immediately. It is a scam.
3. **Enable biometric authentication and 2FA:** Always configure fingerprint or FaceID inside your official banking app.
