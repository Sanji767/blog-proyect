# Best Bank Accounts for Teens & Kids in Europe (2026)

More and more teenagers are opening their own bank accounts to receive allowance money, save for goals, make online purchases, or manage their first part-time earnings. However, not all accounts are tailored for minors, and conditions vary significantly across European banks.

In this guide by **FinanzasEU.com**, we explain how teen bank accounts work, what minors can do with them, what parents and legal guardians must keep in mind, and the most important features to compare.

---

## What is a Teen Bank Account?

A youth account is designed specifically for minors who have not yet reached legal adulthood (typically between **12 and 17 years old**, though some apps allow accounts from age 6).

Key features typically include:
* **Receiving money:** Automated allowance or family transfers.
* **Debit Card:** Physical and virtual contactless cards.
* **Online & In-store Purchases:** With parent-supervised 3D Secure verification.
* **ATM Withdrawals:** With adjustable daily limits.
* **Savings Pockets / Goals:** Visual savings trackers with round-up features.
* **Parental Controls:** Real-time push alerts, spending limits, and 1-tap card locking.
* **Financial Education:** Practical budgeting skills from an early age.

## 5 Essential Tips for Parents

1. **Automate allowance:** Set a fixed weekly or monthly transfer so teens learn to budget independently.
2. **Teach the 50/30/20 rule:** 60% for daily wants, 30% for medium-term savings, and 10% for personal goals.
3. **Monthly spending reviews:** Review the app's visual spending breakdowns together constructively.
4. **Beware of subscriptions:** Audit recurring app and gaming subscriptions regularly.
5. **Digital security:** Remind teens never to share PINs, passwords, or SMS verification codes.
