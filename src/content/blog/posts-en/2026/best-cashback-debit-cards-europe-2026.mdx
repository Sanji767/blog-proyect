## 🏆 Top Cashback Programs Reviewed

### 1. Trade Republic — 1% Saveback Invested in Global ETFs
Trade Republic's Saveback automatically invests **1% of all debit card transactions** into a selected stock or ETF (e.g., MSCI World or S&P 500).

- **How it works:** Spending €1,000/month on groceries, utilities, and dining earns **€10/month invested automatically**.
- **Requirement:** Maintain an active recurring investment plan of at least €50/month.

### 2. Trading 212 — Instant Cash Refunds
Trading 212 offers up to **1.5% cashback** on debit card spending paid directly into your uninvested cash balance.
