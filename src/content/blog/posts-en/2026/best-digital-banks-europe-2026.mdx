# Top 8 Digital Banks in Europe for 2026

Digital banking in Europe has completely transformed how expats, digital nomads, and European residents manage their money. Traditional brick-and-mortar banks with heavy monthly fees and slow customer service are rapidly losing ground to agile neobanks.

In this guide, we break down the top European digital banks for 2026 based on fees, security, IBAN flexibility, and user experience.

---

## 1. Revolut – Best Overall Multi-Currency Account
- **IBAN**: LT (Lithuanian European IBAN)
- **Monthly Fee**: €0 (Standard Plan)
- **Key Advantage**: Zero-fee currency exchange on weekdays across 25+ currencies, virtual disposable cards, and instant stock/crypto trading.

## 2. N26 – Best German IBAN & Deposit Protection
- **IBAN**: DE (German IBAN)
- **Monthly Fee**: €0
- **Key Advantage**: Full German banking license with €100,000 BaFin deposit guarantee and a sleek, fast mobile app.

## 3. Wise – Best for International Transfers
- **IBAN**: BE (Belgian IBAN)
- **Monthly Fee**: €0
- **Key Advantage**: Mid-market exchange rate without hidden markups. Essential for receiving multi-currency salary payments.

## 4. Bunq – Best for Freelancers & Sub-accounts
- **IBAN**: NL (Dutch IBAN)
- **Monthly Fee**: From €2.99/mo
- **Key Advantage**: Create up to 25 separate sub-accounts, each with its own IBAN for automated budgeting.

## 5. Trade Republic – Best for Savings & 4% Interest
- **IBAN**: DE (German IBAN)
- **Monthly Fee**: €0
- **Key Advantage**: Earn high interest on uninvested cash with full European deposit protection.

---

## How to Choose the Right Neobank for You
- **For Travel & FX**: Choose Revolut or Wise.
- **For Salary Domiciliation in EU**: Choose N26 or Bunq.
- **For High Interest Savings**: Choose Trade Republic.
