## ⚠️ Critical Rule: Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC)

When paying at a card machine in a foreign country, the terminal may prompt: *"Charge in your home currency (EUR)"* or *"Charge in local currency (USD/JPY/GBP)"*.

**ALWAYS CHOOSE THE LOCAL CURRENCY.** If you select your home currency, the local merchant's bank will apply its own inflated conversion rate (often adding up to 8% in hidden fees).
