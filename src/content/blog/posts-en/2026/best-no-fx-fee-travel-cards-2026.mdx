# Top No-FX-Fee Debit Cards for International Travel (2026)

Using a traditional bank card abroad can incur hidden foreign exchange fees ranging from **3% to 5% per payment**. Dedicated travel debit cards provide real-time currency exchange with zero markups.

### 1. Revolut — Real Interbank Exchange Rates
Revolut offers fee-free currency exchange up to €1,000/month across 30+ currencies and up to €200/month in fee-free ATM withdrawals globally.

### 2. Wise — Hold 40+ Currencies Simultaneously
Wise is ideal for multi-country trips, enabling users to convert and store local currencies in advance using real mid-market exchange rates.

### 3. N26 — Unlimited Free Foreign Currency Card Payments
N26 charges **0% foreign transaction fees** on all debit card purchases worldwide with no monthly spending limits.
