# The €10,000 Savings Experiment: A Visual Guide to Europe's Best Savings Accounts (2026)

Meet **Mark** and **Elena**. Both have saved an emergency cushion of **€10,000** through hard work.

- **Mark** leaves his money in a traditional high-street checking account paying **€0.00 interest** while charging monthly account fees.
- **Elena** takes 5 minutes to transfer her savings into a high-yield account paying **3.75% APY** with zero monthly fees and 100% daily liquidity.

![Compound interest growth chart](/images/blog/savings_interest_chart.jpg)

### 📈 After 1 Year:
- **Mark:** Kept €9,940 (after fees) and lost over **€250 to inflation**.
- **Elena:** Now has **€10,375 net in her account**, earning **€375 in passive income** for zero extra work.
