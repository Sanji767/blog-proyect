# How to Get Out of Debt Fast

Debt can feel overwhelming, but following a structured plan can help you become debt-free faster than you think.

## Step 1: List All Your Debts
Create a simple spreadsheet detailing:
- Total balance owed
- Minimum monthly payment
- Annual Percentage Rate (APR)

## Step 2: Choose Your Payoff Strategy

### Option A: The Debt Avalanche Method (Mathematically Superior)
- Pay minimums on all debts.
- Throw all extra money at the debt with the **highest interest rate**.
- Saves the most money in interest over time.

### Option B: The Debt Snowball Method (Psychologically Superior)
- Pay minimums on all debts.
- Throw all extra money at the debt with the **smallest total balance**.
- Gives quick emotional wins to keep you motivated.

## Step 3: Negotiate Lower Interest Rates
Call your card issuers or lenders and ask for a temporary interest rate reduction or balance transfer to a 0% APR card.
