# Revolut Review 2026: Is It Safe? Fees, Plans & Honest Verdict

With [over 70 million customers worldwide](https://jeangalea.com/revolut-review/), Revolut remains the leading digital financial app in Europe. But is it right for your primary bank account or travel budget?

## Key Features
- **Multi-Currency Holding**: Hold and exchange over 30 currencies instantly.
- **Disposable Virtual Cards**: Generate new card numbers for one-time online shopping to prevent fraud.
- **Vaults & Savings**: Earn competitive interest rates on daily spare change.

## Pricing Plans Comparison
Prices below are for Germany and vary by country of residence — check the Revolut app for local pricing. ([Source](https://voyagehacks.com/en/credit-cards/revolut/travel-card/))

1. **Standard (€0/mo)**: Free card, zero FX fee up to €1,000/mo, €200 free ATM withdrawals.
2. **Plus (€2.99/mo)**: Priority support, purchase protection, higher limits.
3. **Premium (€8.99/mo)**: Unlimited currency exchange, global medical insurance, virtual cards.
4. **Metal (€15.99/mo)**: Cashback on purchases, 18 g solid steel card, delayed flight insurance.
5. **Ultra (€65/mo)**: Unlimited lounge access, highest ATM limits, comprehensive travel insurance.

## Pros & Cons
**Pros**:
- Mid-market exchange rates without markup on weekdays.
- High app security with geolocation protection.
- Instant transfers between Revolut users.

**Cons**:
- 1% weekend markup on currency exchanges.
- Customer support is chat-only.

## Verdict
Revolut is an absolute must-have for travellers, digital nomads, and online shoppers.
