# Revolut Review 2026: The Ultimate European Neobank?

With over 45 million customers worldwide, Revolut remains the leading digital financial app in Europe. But is it right for your primary bank account or travel budget?

## Key Features
- **Multi-Currency Holding**: Hold and exchange over 25 currencies instantly.
- **Disposable Virtual Cards**: Generate new card numbers for one-time online shopping to prevent fraud.
- **Vaults & Savings**: Earn competitive interest rates on daily spare change.

## Pricing Plans Comparison
1. **Standard (€0/mo)**: Free card, zero FX fee up to €1,000/mo, €200 free ATM withdrawals.
2. **Premium (€8.99/mo)**: Unlimited currency exchange, global medical insurance, virtual cards.
3. **Metal (€15.99/mo)**: Cashback on purchases, 1kg solid steel card, delayed flight insurance.

## Pros & Cons
**Pros**:
- Mid-market exchange rates without markup on weekdays.
- High app security with geolocation protection.
- Instant transfers between Revolut users.

**Cons**:
- 1% weekend markup on currency exchanges.
- Customer support is chat-only.

Revolut is an absolute must-have for travellers, digital nomads, and online shoppers.
