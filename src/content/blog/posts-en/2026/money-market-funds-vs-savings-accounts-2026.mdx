# Money Market Funds vs. Savings Accounts: 2026 European Guide

As central bank rates adjust, **Money Market Funds (MMFs)** have become a popular low-risk alternative to traditional fixed-term bank deposits across Europe.

---

## 📈 What is a Money Market Fund?

An MMF is a fund investing in ultra-short-term government debt (such as French or German treasury bills) and top-rated corporate commercial paper. They offer daily liquidity and returns benchmarked to ECB central bank interest rates.
