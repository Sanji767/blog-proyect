# DEGIRO Review 2026: Still Europe's Cheapest Broker for Buy-and-Hold ETF Investors

DEGIRO has been Europe's low-cost broker of reference for over a decade, and in 2026 it still earns that reputation — for a specific kind of investor. If you buy broad-market ETFs once a month and hold them for years, DEGIRO is hard to beat: a €1 handling fee per trade on its Core Selection, no custody fee, no inactivity fee and no account fee. If you want automation, fractional shares or interest on your cash, it will frustrate you.

## What DEGIRO is

DEGIRO is a Netherlands-based discount broker, launched in 2013, now operating as the Dutch branch of flatexDEGIRO Bank SE — a German bank supervised by BaFin, with the Dutch branch additionally overseen by De Nederlandsche Bank (DNB) and the Dutch Authority for the Financial Markets (AFM). Its parent group, flatexDEGIRO, had [3.66 million customer accounts and €108.3 billion in assets under custody at the end of June 2026](https://investingintheweb.com/reviews/degiro-review/).

## Fees: the Core Selection is the whole story

DEGIRO's pricing is built around one list: the ETF Core Selection. In October 2025 DEGIRO overhauled it, [expanding the selection to over 1,000 products — ETFs, ETCs and ETNs — traded on the Tradegate exchange, removing the connectivity fee on those products and simplifying the rules](https://www.eupersonalfinance.eu/articles/degiro-free-etfs). A €1 handling fee per transaction still applies, charged by an external entity. The list includes portfolio building blocks such as the Vanguard S&P 500 UCITS ETF, the iShares Core MSCI World UCITS ETF (IWDA, 0.20% TER) and the Vanguard FTSE All-World UCITS ETF (VWCE, 0.14% TER) — but only their Tradegate listings qualify.

The rest of the fee schedule, verified against DEGIRO's fee schedules in 2026:

| ETF outside the Core Selection | €2 + €1 handling fee |
| US and Canadian shares | €1 + €1 handling fee |
| European shares | €3.90 + €1 handling fee (varies by country entity) |

Sources: [DEGIRO review, fees as of August 2026](https://www.eupersonalfinance.eu/articles/degiro-review), [DEGIRO fee verification, October 2026](https://www.steadfolio.eu/brokers/degiro.html).

Two fees deserve attention because they are easy to miss. The **connectivity fee** is €2.50 a year per foreign exchange you invest on, capped at 0.25% of your account value — whichever is lower. It does not apply to your domestic market or to Core Selection ETFs on Tradegate, so a buy-and-hold investor with one or two ETFs may never pay it. The **0.25% AutoFX fee** applies every time you buy a non-euro asset — on a €1,000 purchase of US shares, that is €2.50, more than the €2 commission itself.

What DEGIRO does not charge is equally important: there is no account fee, no custody fee, no inactivity fee and no withdrawal fee. An account can sit untouched for a year and cost nothing.

## What it costs in practice

Take a typical monthly ETF investor putting €500 a month into a Core Selection world ETF on Tradegate. Each trade costs €1 — €12 a year on €6,000 invested — plus the ETF's own running costs (for example, 0.20% a year for IWDA). There is no connectivity fee on the Core Selection and no custody fee.

The picture changes off-list. The same €500 into a non-Core ETF costs €3 a trade plus a possible €2.50 annual connectivity fee. Buying US shares costs €2 a trade plus 0.25% FX — €4.50 on a €1,000 order. Checking the Core Selection before every trade is worth the habit.

## Accounts: Basic is now the only option for new clients

DEGIRO historically offered two account types that mattered to long-term investors. The **Basic** account was cheaper but allowed DEGIRO to lend out your shares to third parties. The **Custody** account cost more — particularly on dividends — but kept your securities ring-fenced and unlent, with no securities lending and no use by third parties. DEGIRO's own documentation describes Custody as "the most secure profile offered by DEGIRO".

That choice is gone for new clients. [DEGIRO closed the Custody account to new clients in 2021, so anyone signing up today gets Basic](https://financialexpertclass.com/degiro-review/). Existing Custody holders keep their original terms, but new investors cannot opt into the more protective structure. The good news is that the gap has narrowed: [since October 2025, securities lending is off by default and opt-in only](https://quantroutine.com/brokers/degiro/), available only in the Netherlands, Spain, Switzerland and Italy. Your shares will not be lent unless you sign the securities-lending acceptance form and pass an appropriateness test — and if you do opt in, DEGIRO pays 50% of net lending revenue to you.

Above Basic sit the Active, Trader and Day Trader profiles, which unlock options, futures, short selling and margin lending. Availability now varies by country: in several markets these advanced profiles are only open to existing clients. For a buy-and-hold ETF investor, none of this matters — Basic is the right profile.

## Safety: segregation, €20,000 investor compensation, €100,000 cash protection

DEGIRO's safety story has three layers, and they are often confused, so here they are separately.

**Asset segregation.** Your investments are held by a separate legal entity (SPV) whose sole task is to administer and safeguard client assets. [Should something happen to DEGIRO, your investments will not be treated as recoverable assets to DEGIRO's creditors and will remain in the safekeeping of the separate entity](https://www.degiro.com/uk/about-degiro/strong-and-reliable). This is the real protection for a long-term investor: your ETFs are your property, not DEGIRO's, and would be identified and transferred to another institution if the broker failed.

**Investor compensation.** In the unlikely event that segregated assets cannot be returned to clients — for example after fraud or an administrative failure — [DEGIRO falls under the German Investor Compensation Scheme, which compensates losses from non-returned assets up to 90%, with a maximum of €20,000](https://www.degiro.com/uk/about-degiro/strong-and-reliable). The €20,000 figure sounds low next to a six-figure portfolio, but it is a backstop for a rare failure, not the first line of defence — segregation is.

**Cash protection.** For clients onboarded from 1 April 2024, money is held in a cash account with flatexDEGIRO Bank SE and [guaranteed up to €100,000 under the German Deposit Guarantee Scheme](https://www.degiro.com/uk/about-degiro/strong-and-reliable). Clients from before April 2024 have their cash held in a money market fund instead, which carries no deposit guarantee.

One more safety note: unlike some competitors, [DEGIRO pays no interest on uninvested cash](https://www.eupersonalfinance.eu/articles/degiro-review). Cash sitting in your account earns 0%. Keep as little idle cash as possible.

## Honest downsides

- **No fractional shares.** Every purchase is for a whole share or ETF unit. If you invest a fixed €100 a month into an ETF trading at €90, €10 sits idle earning 0%.
- **No savings plans or auto-invest.** Every trade is placed manually. There is no set-and-forget monthly automation, which is now standard at Trade Republic and others.
- **0% interest on cash.** Uninvested money earns nothing, so every euro left idle is a euro losing value to inflation.
- **The 0.25% FX fee adds up.** On US shares it can exceed the commission itself, and it applies on both the buy and the sell.
- **The Custody account is closed to new clients.** The most protective account structure is no longer available — you get Basic, with securities lending off by default but the choice itself gone.
- **Core Selection only works on Tradegate.** The same ETF on Xetra or Euronext costs more, and Tradegate positions are not accepted by every broker on an incoming transfer, which can complicate switching later.
- **Transferring out costs €20 per position.** Leaving DEGIRO with ten positions costs €200 plus external costs — a real switching cost worth knowing before you build a large portfolio.
- **Crypto is limited.** [Eligible crypto is available at 0.29% plus spread (0.50% in Germany, France and Ireland)](https://cryptotips.eu/en/review/degiro/), but assets cannot be transferred to a personal wallet, staking is unavailable, and crypto is excluded from the German investor compensation scheme.

## Who should open DEGIRO in 2026?

DEGIRO is the right broker if you buy ETFs manually, stick to the Core Selection, and want the lowest possible explicit costs with access to 45+ exchanges. It is the wrong broker if you want automation — Trade Republic's free savings plans are the better tool — if you invest small fixed amounts where fractional shares matter, or if you keep significant cash uninvested and want it to earn interest. And if you trade US stocks frequently, run the numbers on the 0.25% FX fee first: on large orders, it quietly becomes your biggest cost.
