# Best Free Business Bank Accounts for Freelancers & SMEs in Europe (2026)

Separating your personal and professional finances is crucial for clean bookkeeping and tax compliance. Modern European fintechs provide **no-fee business bank accounts tailored for freelancers and sole traders**.

---

## 🏆 Top Digital Business Accounts in 2026

### 1. Qonto — Invoicing & Accounting Integration
Qonto offers digital business banking with sub-accounts, expense receipt scanning, automated tax categorization, and direct integration with major accounting tools.

### 2. SumUp Business — Card Reader & Free Business Account
SumUp combines a zero-monthly-fee business checking account with low transaction fee card readers and instant payout facilities.

### 3. N26 Business — 0.1% Cashback on All Purchases
N26 Business is a 100% free account for sole proprietors offering **0.1% cashback** on every debit card purchase and built-in budgeting spaces.

### 4. Revolut Business — Multi-Currency Invoicing for Global Clients
Best for freelancers billing overseas clients in USD, GBP, or EUR with zero markup foreign exchange rates.
