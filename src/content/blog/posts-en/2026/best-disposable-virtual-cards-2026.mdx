# Best Disposable Virtual Cards for Secure Online Shopping in 2026

Shopping on new websites or signing up for free trials can expose your primary debit card details. **Single-use disposable virtual cards** generate new card credentials for every single payment, completely protecting your main bank account from fraud.

---

## 🔒 How Disposable Virtual Cards Work

Unlike standard digital cards, a **disposable virtual card** auto-destructs its 16-digit card number, CVV, and expiration date immediately after a transaction completes. A fresh card is automatically created in seconds for your next purchase.

---

## 🏆 Top Banks Offering Virtual Cards in 2026

### 1. Revolut — Unlimited Single-Use Disposable Cards
Revolut provides **unlimited disposable virtual cards** for free across all accounts. The moment a charge is approved, the card number is discarded and regenerated.

### 2. Wise — Multi-Currency Digital Cards
Wise allows you to generate up to 3 active virtual cards connected to your multi-currency balances, perfect for paying abroad in 40+ currencies at real mid-market exchange rates.

### 3. N26 — Virtual Cards for Apple & Google Pay
N26 offers instant digital cards assigned to sub-account Spaces, preventing unauthorized subscription renewals.
