# Best Disposable Virtual Cards for Secure Online Shopping in 2026

Shopping on new websites or signing up for free trials can expose your primary debit card details. **Single-use disposable virtual cards** generate new card credentials for every single payment, completely protecting your main bank account from fraud.

The risk is not theoretical. Card payment fraud in the European Economic Area [reached €1.329 billion in 2024, up 29% on the year before](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2025/html/ecb.pr251215~e133d9d683.en.html), according to the joint EBA-ECB report on payment fraud — and remote (online) card payments made up only about 28% of card payment value but around 83% of card fraud. The fraud rate on remote card payments was 13 times higher than for in-person payments. A disposable card number is one of the simplest ways to shrink that exposure to near zero.

---

## How Disposable Virtual Cards Work

Unlike standard digital cards, a **disposable virtual card** auto-destructs its 16-digit card number, CVV, and expiration date immediately after a transaction completes. A fresh card is automatically created in seconds for your next purchase.

There are two distinct designs, and the difference matters:

- **True single-use (auto-rotating) cards.** The details change automatically after every payment. You never reuse a number, so a leaked number is useless by definition. Revolut is the main European provider of this type.
- **Replaceable virtual cards.** You get a reusable virtual card that you can delete and replace manually at any time. The number stays valid until you replace it, so you control the rotation. Wise and N26 work this way.

Both designs beat using your physical card online. The single-use version is stronger for one-off purchases on unfamiliar sites; the replaceable version is more practical for subscriptions you want to keep running but isolate from your main card.

## Top Banks Offering Virtual Cards in 2026

### 1. Revolut — Free Single-Use Disposable Cards
Revolut provides **free disposable virtual cards on every plan, including Standard**. You hold one single-use card at a time: the moment a charge is approved, the card number is discarded and a fresh one is regenerated for your next purchase. ([Source: Revolut](https://www.revolut.com/en-US/blog/post/how-to-get-a-virtual-card/))

Revolut is the only major European bank offering a true auto-rotating disposable card for free. Alongside the disposable card, you can hold up to 20 reusable virtual cards, with up to 5 disposable-card payments every 24 hours. The disposable card is ideal for one-off purchases, free trials and any site you do not fully trust — but do not use it for anything that needs to charge you again later (a hotel deposit, a car rental hold, a subscription), because the number will already be dead when the second charge arrives.

### 2. Wise — Multi-Currency Digital Cards
Wise allows you to generate up to 3 active virtual cards connected to your multi-currency balances, perfect for paying abroad in 40+ currencies at real mid-market exchange rates.

Wise's digital cards are free, and you can [delete and replace them up to 3 times a day](https://wise.com/help/articles/1AqrRndtxosydM2GLR3U6T/getting-started-with-your-wise-digital-card?origin=related-article-2970190) — which gives you disposable-style protection with manual control. A practical setup: dedicate one card to subscriptions, one to online shopping, and keep one spare. If a merchant is breached, delete that card and the exposure ends there; your physical card and your other virtual cards are untouched. Note that you need to order a physical card first (a one-off €7 fee in the EEA) before you can create digital cards, and the digital card works with Apple Pay and Google Pay for in-store contactless spending too.

### 3. N26 — Virtual Cards for Apple & Google Pay
N26 offers instant digital cards assigned to sub-account Spaces, preventing unauthorized subscription renewals.

N26's approach is different again: virtual cards are tied to Spaces, so you can give a subscription its own Space with its own card and its own budget. If a trial converts to a paid plan you did not want, the money simply is not there — the Space caps the damage. N26's free Standard plan includes a virtual card, and higher plans add more Spaces and cards. Combined with instant freeze and real-time notifications in the app, it is a solid setup for subscription management rather than for one-off anonymous purchases.

## When Disposable Cards Do Not Help

Virtual cards protect your card *number*. They do not protect you from everything:

- **Authorised push payment scams.** If a scammer tricks you into sending a bank transfer yourself, no card product helps — and in 2024, payment service users bore [approximately 85% of credit transfer fraud losses](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2025/html/ecb.pr251215~e133d9d683.en.html) in the EEA. Never transfer money to a "safe account" at someone's request.
- **Phishing.** A fake login page captures your banking credentials, not your card number. Our guide to [protecting your bank account from phishing](/en/blog/how-to-protect-bank-account-phishing-2026) covers vishing, smishing and your refund rights under PSD2.
- **Deposits and holds.** Hotels, car rental firms and some marketplaces place a hold and charge later. A disposable number will fail the second charge — use a reusable virtual card for these.
- **Refunds.** A refund to a dead disposable number usually still reaches your account (refunds follow the original transaction), but some merchants refuse to refund to a different card, so keep the receipt and the transaction reference.

1. **Open a free Revolut Standard account** and generate your disposable card for one-off online purchases.
2. **Open a free Wise account**, order the physical card, and create three digital cards: one for subscriptions, one for shopping, one spare.

## FAQ

**Are virtual cards safe for large purchases?**
Yes — they carry the same Mastercard/Visa protections and chargeback rights as a physical card. For very large purchases, a credit card may offer stronger chargeback protection than a debit card; see our guide to the [best rewards credit cards](/en/blog/best-rewards-credit-cards-2026).

**Can I use a disposable card for a free trial?**
Yes, and it is one of the best uses: if you forget to cancel, the merchant cannot charge a dead number. Just make sure you are not contractually obliged to pay (for example, an insurance policy with a minimum term).

**Do disposable cards work with 3D Secure?**
Yes. Single-use and virtual cards go through the same Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) as physical cards — you approve the payment in the app as normal.
