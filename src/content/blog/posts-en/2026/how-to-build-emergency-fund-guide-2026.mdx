# How to Build a 3-to-6 Month Emergency Fund Strategy in 2026

An **emergency fund** is a dedicated pool of cash set aside exclusively for unexpected financial shocks: job loss, urgent home repairs, medical emergencies, or car breakdowns.

---

## 🧮 1. Calculate Your Essential Monthly Expenses

Your emergency fund size depends on **unavoidable monthly living costs**, not your total gross income:

1. Rent / Mortgage & Utilities (power, water, internet)
2. Groceries & essential food
3. Debt obligations & insurance
4. Essential transport

> **Formula Example:** `Essential Monthly Expense = €1,500`
> - **Minimum Cushion (3 Months):** €4,500
> - **Optimal Cushion (6 Months):** €9,000

---

## 🏦 2. Where to Keep Your Emergency Fund in 2026

An emergency fund requires **100% daily liquidity and zero capital risk**. Never invest your emergency fund in stocks, real estate, or crypto.

### Top Options in 2026:
- **High-Yield Savings Accounts (Trade Republic 3.75% APY / N26 2.80% APY):** Interest paid daily/monthly with Deposit Guarantee Scheme (DGS) protection up to €100,000.
