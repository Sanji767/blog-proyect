# Wise Review 2026: Transparent International Banking

Wise (formerly TransferWise) is globally famous for eliminating hidden bank fees on international transfers.

## Why Wise Stands Out
- **Real Mid-Market Rate**: Wise uses the exact exchange rate you see on Google.
- **Local Account Details**: Get local bank details for EUR, USD, GBP, AUD, CAD, and 5+ other currencies.
- **Wise Debit Card**: Spend money in 40+ currencies seamlessly while travelling.

## Fees Structure
- **Account Opening**: €0
- **Monthly Maintenance**: €0
- **Transfer Fee**: Variable transparent fee starting from 0.33%.

## Ideal For
Expatriates, remote workers, freelancers receiving payments from foreign clients, and international travellers.
